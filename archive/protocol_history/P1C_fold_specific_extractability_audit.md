# P1C Fold-Specific Extractability Audit

## Scope and protocol

Technical-only audit of the frozen W06 A modeling population and frozen W07 outer splits.
For each repeat/fold, the habitat boundary was fitted from outer-training patients only with patient-balanced K=2 and applied to the corresponding training and validation cases using frozen SLIC labels and supervoxel means.

- Outer folds scanned: 10 repeats × 5 folds = 50/50.
- Population: 393 A cases; frozen W07 role sizes are 314/79 or 315/78 (training/validation) according to the locked split rows.
- State 0: 0 mask voxels; state 1: 1–9 mask voxels; state 2: ≥10 mask voxels.
- `n_dual_ge10` is the number of cases with both R_low and R_high support ≥10; role-specific values are reported separately.
- The locked 10-voxel minimum support rule, thresholds, SLIC labels, supervoxel means, and W07 splits were unchanged.
- B_data_read=false. No radiomics extraction, Cox fit, prediction, performance statistic, or model selection was executed.

## Timing

The accepted 1 repeat × 3 fold probe measured 1.384 seconds for setup, then 139.273 / 26.378 / 29.066 seconds for folds 1 / 2 / 3. With the same in-process case-cache semantics, the estimate was 1499.043 seconds (24.98 minutes); the completed 50-fold scan took 1846.920 seconds.

## Technical conclusion

The 50 fixed fold-specific boundaries were applied without changing the frozen split plan or the 10-voxel support rule. The reported state counts quantify structural absence, technically unextractable small support, and radiomics-extractable support for R_low and R_high in the training and validation roles. This audit addresses fold-specific boundary and mask-support sensitivity only and does not authorize fold exclusion, threshold changes, or formal modeling.
